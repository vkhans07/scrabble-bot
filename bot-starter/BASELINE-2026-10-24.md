Hypotheses for later controlled experiments: bid pacing and the fixed face-value opponent model may limit scoring. Future opponent balances remain constant in rollouts, and native continuation cash-ins still approximate the live policy. None of these is established as the cause of the observed placements. No speculative policy tuning was made during this sample.

Validation: 29 bot tests passed, including the compiled native cash-in regression; TypeScript no-emit check and native build passed. Full SDK integration was exercised by these live matches, not exhaustively tested.
